# **App Name**: FestaPay

## Core Features:

- Card Creation & Activation: Generate a unique QR code ID and creates a digital card in the system, assigning an initial balance.
- Balance Recharge: Add funds to an existing digital card by scanning its QR code and specifying the recharge amount.
- Transaction Processing: Process sales by scanning the user's QR code and debiting the specified amount from their balance. Manage approval and error conditions
- Balance Inquiry: Allow users or vendors to quickly check the balance of a digital card by scanning the QR code.
- Sales Report Summarization: Tool to automatically analyze sales data and generate reports, identifying trends and insights for event organizers.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep violet (#6A479F), inspired by the concepts of security, reliability, and currency management. A rich hue associated with trust and value, without being a direct reference to money itself.
- Background color: Very light violet (#F2EEF7) offers a muted, clean backdrop that complements the depth of the primary violet.
- Accent color: Lilac (#B19CD8), brighter and less saturated than the primary, it highlights key interactive elements without overpowering the interface.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a sans-serif font known for its modern and neutral aesthetic, ensuring readability and clarity throughout the app. Its clean lines and balanced proportions are perfect for both headings and body text.
- Use clear and intuitive icons for navigation and actions, ensuring ease of use for both vendors and event staff.
- Maintain a clean and organized layout across all application interfaces.
- Use subtle animations to indicate transaction success, errors, or loading states.
